Gildas Ngouana Melachi was born in 2000 in Cameroon. He holds a Master's degree in Electronics, Radiofrequency and Telecommunications from the Université de Bretagne Occidentale (UBO), obtained in 2025. He is currently pursuing a PhD at INSA Rennes, focusing on conformal metasurfaces applied to curved-geometry objects, with applications in military aeronautics.
